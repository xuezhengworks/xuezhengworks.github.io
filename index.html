About

Building trustworthy collaboration into machine learning.

I am a post-candidacy Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Aylin Yener in the INSPIRE@OhioState Lab. My work spans algorithm design, theoretical analysis, and systems evaluation for federated and decentralized learning.

I am especially interested in settings where institutions or edge devices cannot share local data or model information directly, yet still need to train and deploy advanced AI systems together.

Questions I work on

01

Federated & decentralized learning

Learning across heterogeneous clients without centralizing their data.

02

Privacy-preserving collaboration

Secure aggregation and peer-to-peer methods that protect local models and information.

03

Distribution shifts

Robust and performative learning when deployed models influence future data.

04

Foundation models & MoE

Resource-aware federated fine-tuning and sparse expert assignment for large models.
